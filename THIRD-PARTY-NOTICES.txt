Fonts and color profiles
------------------------
F Reader does not ship Microsoft fonts or color profiles. When you choose to embed text or make a PDF/A file, it reads
Arial and the sRGB profile from your own copy of Windows, only to write them into your document where Microsoft's font
and profile terms permit that.
